Service areas

Desert Ridge, north Phoenix and nearby communities.

Concentrating on one part of the Valley keeps consultations prompt and schedules flexible.

Desert Ridge
Our home area spans the 85050 and 85054 ZIP codes around the Loop 101 and SR-51. We coat garages, interiors and terraces in residential neighborhoods and work regularly with offices, medical suites, restaurants and retail in the surrounding commercial district.
Aviano & Fireside
Newer neighborhoods north of the 101 feature modern garages, great rooms that open to the outdoors and covered terraces. Satin interiors, graphite flake garages and light exterior finishes are frequent requests.
Tatum Ranch
Established homes and golf-course properties along Tatum Boulevard often have larger garages and mature outdoor spaces. Many projects here involve removing older coatings or paint before installing a new system.
Kierland
Around the Kierland corridor we work with condo owners, boutique retail and offices, where refined finishes and careful scheduling matter in shared buildings.
North Scottsdale
To the east, Grayhawk, DC Ranch and nearby communities include collector garages, contemporary interiors and expansive terraces suited to premium finishes.
Paradise Valley Village
South of the 101 along Tatum and Cave Creek Road, this Phoenix village includes a mix of homes from several decades. Older slabs may need moisture mitigation or additional preparation.
Cave Creek Road corridor
Heading north, homes and light-commercial properties along Cave Creek Road benefit from durable garage and shop floors.
Deer Valley
West along the 101, Deer Valley’s industrial parks, hangars and service buildings are where most of our warehouse and fleet work takes place.

Outside these areas?

We travel for larger residential and commercial projects throughout the north Valley. Share your location and project details and we’ll confirm availability. All services — including garage coatings, interior floors and commercial flooring — are offered across our service area.
