About us

A floor studio with a builder’s discipline.

We design and install coated floors for homes and businesses across Desert Ridge and north Phoenix, with preparation treated as the most important part of the job.

Our point of view

Floors in modern architecture carry a lot of visual weight. They’re the largest continuous surface in a room, and they meet glass, cabinetry and stone at every edge. We think that deserves the same care given to millwork or tile. At the same time, a coating is a performance system: it has to bond, resist wear and stay true in Arizona sun. Our work sits at the intersection of those two ideas.

In practice that means we spend more time than most on the parts nobody sees. Slabs are diamond-ground with dust extraction. Repairs are feathered so they disappear under satin and metallic finishes. Moisture is measured, not assumed. And every layer of the system is chosen deliberately and written down in the proposal.

How we work with clients

  • Consultations on site, with physical samples evaluated in your own light.
  • Written proposals that describe preparation, repairs and each coating layer.
  • Surface-only preparation that respects post-tension foundations.
  • Coordination with designers, architects, contractors and property managers.
  • Clean, protected job sites and clear timelines for return to use.

Who we serve

Homeowners make up much of our work — garages, interiors, terraces and casitas in Desert Ridge and the surrounding neighborhoods. We also serve the area’s commercial community: offices, medical suites, showrooms, salons and restaurants, along with warehouses and service buildings toward Deer Valley.

If you’re still researching, our service pages explain each system in detail, including where it fits and where it doesn’t. Questions are always welcome.
